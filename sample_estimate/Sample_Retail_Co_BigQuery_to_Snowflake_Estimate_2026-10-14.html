Assumptions and caveats

  • 16 BigQuery slots = 1 X-Small Snowflake node is a hardware comparison, not an exact performance match. Actual throughput varies by workload (ETL, BI, ad hoc).
  • The baseline averages 7 months of usage. Short observation periods, one-time loads, or seasonal peaks outside the window make the estimate less reliable.
  • Utilization % is calculated across every project-month that had activity. Months where a project ran no queries are not included.
  • Prices are list-price defaults unless marked as customer values in Inputs.
  • The 80% / 120% range reflects estimation uncertainty and should be widened if the source data is incomplete.
